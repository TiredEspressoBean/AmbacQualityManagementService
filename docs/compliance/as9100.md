# AS9100D — Clause Support

Which AS9100D aerospace additions uqmes performs, which it evidences, and which remain yours.

## Overview

AS9100D builds on ISO 9001 with aerospace-specific requirements. This page
covers the aerospace additions — see [ISO 9001](iso9001.md) for the base clauses
and for how the markers below are used.

!!! info "AS9100 is becoming IA9100"
    The aerospace standard is being reissued as **IA9100** — *International
    Aerospace* — a single global standard aligned with **ISO 9001:2026**, with
    publication planned for 2026. It is not out yet, so **AS9100D remains the
    standard you are certified to** and this page maps it.

    Expect the ISO 9001:2026 changes to flow through: the quality-culture and
    ethics additions, the risk and opportunity split, and the extension of change
    management across the life of a change — see [ISO 9001](iso9001.md). The
    aerospace-specific clauses mapped below are not expected to move much.

    Watch the designation rather than assuming "Rev E": the sector's configuration
    practice may reset it instead.

There is no readiness percentage here: AS9100 certifies **your organisation**,
and the aerospace additions lean heavily on configuration discipline and
first-article evidence, which is where uqmes does its work.

| Standard | Version | Where uqmes sits |
|----------|---------|------------------|
| AS9100 | Rev D | Provides the production, traceability and configuration-control machinery; the programme around it is yours |
| AS9102 | Rev C | First-article inspection data is captured and reportable; the AS9102 Forms 1–3 output itself is not generated |

### 8.5 Production and Service Provision

| Clause | Requirement | Status | Evidence | Gaps / Notes |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------|-------|
| 8.5.1.1 | Equipment/tools/software | ✅ | The **Equipment + roles** capture records which machine and gauge were used against each part at a step; inspection equipment is linked to the quality report it measured (`QualityReportEquipment`), which is what the calibration exposure list reads |  |
| 8.5.1.2 | Special process validation | 🟡 | A supplier qualification can be scoped to a **special process** — heat treat, plating, NDT — with a certificate basis such as Nadcap and an expiry. An outside-process step naming a **Special process** refuses send-out to a vendor without an active qualification for it | The check runs only where the step names its special process. Validation of your own in-house special processes is not modelled |
| 8.5.1.3 | Production process verification | ✅ | `is_first_piece` flag; `requires_first_piece_inspection` on Steps; FPI workflow with scope options |  |
| 8.5.2 | Identification and traceability | ✅ | Parts have serial/ERP IDs; StepTransitionLog with operator/timestamp. **Acceptance authority media:** a signature block in a work instruction is the electronic equivalent of an inspection stamp, and the controls over it are the ones this clause asks you to document — who may sign (permissions), that a verifier cannot be the person who did the work (**enforced without exception** — the check refuses outright when verifier and submitter are the same account, and the finder is stamped by the server as the submitter), re-authentication with the signer's own password where the author requires it, attribution set by the server rather than the client, a throttle on failed attempts, and retraction only by the signer before submission or by a supervisor holding `clear_gate_signature`, with the retracted signature kept. See [Electronic Signatures](signatures.md). |  |
| 8.5.4 | Preservation | ➖ | Physical handling | Storage locations can be restricted (held stock only), and shelf life is tracked on material lots |
| 8.5.5 | Post-delivery activities | 🟡 | CAPA customer complaint type | Gap: Warranty tracking, field failure analysis |
| 8.5.6 | Control of changes | ✅ | Immutable approved processes; version control; change justification |  |

### 8.6 Release of Products

| Clause | Requirement | Status | Evidence |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------|
| 8.6 | Release | ✅ | A step can be gated so a lot does not advance until QA releases it (`requires_qa_signoff`), and the author chooses how firmly that release is signed (`qa_signoff_assurance`: check off, or signature + password). The `QaApproval` carries the signer, the server's time, the originating IP, the verification method, and a server-derived meaning — *"QA release of {step} for {work order}"*. Whoever completed the step's substeps, **on units still at the step**, is refused — and that check runs before the password, so a release cannot be bought by someone not entitled to make it.<br><br>**Scope that claim carefully in an audit.** It is a bar on releasing work you are recorded as having just done, not a guarantee that two people touched every lot: a step with no substeps has no completions to find, completions on units that already moved past the step fall outside it, and so do voided ones. Where you need true independence on every lot, the control is procedural. |

!!! important "Two different controls, both called segregation"
    This page claims segregation of duties in two rows and they are **not the
    same control**. Read them separately in an audit:

    - **8.5.2, the inspection verifier.** Unconditional. A verifying signature
      is refused when the verifier is the person submitting, full stop — no
      scope, no precondition, nothing to work around.
    - **8.6, the QA release.** Bounded, as stated in that row. It bars you from
      releasing work you are recorded as having just done, which is narrower
      than "two people touched this lot".

    The wording is similar because the intent is; the enforcement is not. Do not
    cite the 8.6 bound as a limit on 8.5.2, or 8.5.2's strength as evidence for
    8.6.

## First Article Inspection (AS9102)

AS9102 requires three forms for first article approval.

| Form | Purpose | Status | Evidence | Gaps / Notes |
|------|---------|--------|----------|-------|
| Form 1 | Part Number Accountability | 🟡 | Parts with serial/ERP; Process versioning; FPI workflow | Data is captured; needs an AS9102 Typst template in `Tracker/reports/` |
| Form 2 | Product Accountability | 🟡 | Documents model; drawing references | Data is captured; needs an AS9102 Typst template in `Tracker/reports/` |
| Form 3 | Characteristic Accountability | 🟡 | MeasurementResult with specs; SPC data; StepMeasurementRequirement | Data is captured; needs an AS9102 Typst template in `Tracker/reports/` |

### FPI Implementation

The application supports First Piece Inspection with:

- **Step-level FPI toggle**: `Steps.requires_first_piece_inspection`
- **FPI Scope options**: PER_WORK_ORDER, PER_SHIFT, PER_EQUIPMENT, PER_OPERATOR
- **FPIRecord model**: Tracks status (PENDING, PASSED, FAILED, WAIVED), approval, waiver reasons
- **Batch hold**: Parts wait at step until FPI passes

**Key files**: `Tracker/models/mes_lite.py` (Steps), `Tracker/models/qms.py` (FPIRecord)

### 3D Annotation for FAI

!!! warning "Not a ballooning tool"
    Annotations on a part's 3D model mark **defect locations** against a quality
    report — a point, an error type, a severity and notes. They are not
    characteristic balloons, they carry no characteristic number, and they do
    not produce AS9102 Form 3's characteristic accountability. Do not present
    them as a substitute for a ballooned drawing.

## Nonconformance and Disposition

### Disposition Types

| Type | Code | Use Case |
|------|------|----------|
| Use As Is | USE_AS_IS | Non-conformance doesn't affect function |
| Rework | REWORK | Can be corrected to full conformance |
| Repair | REPAIR | Corrected but may deviate (requires approval) |
| Scrap | SCRAP | Cannot be used or repaired |
| Return to Supplier | RETURN_TO_SUPPLIER | Supplier responsible |

### Customer Approval Workflow

For dispositions requiring customer authorization:

```
┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐
│ Disposition │────▶│  Customer   │────▶│   Execute   │
│  Decision   │     │  Approval   │     │  Decision   │
└─────────────┘     └─────────────┘     └─────────────┘
```

Fields: `requires_customer_approval`, `customer_approval_received`, `customer_approval_reference`, `customer_approval_date`
