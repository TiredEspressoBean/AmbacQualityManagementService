# AS9100D — Clause Support

Which AS9100D aerospace additions uqmes performs, which it evidences, and which remain yours.

## Overview

AS9100D builds on ISO 9001 with aerospace-specific requirements. This page
covers the aerospace additions — see [ISO 9001](iso9001.md) for the base clauses
and for how the markers below are used.

!!! info "AS9100 is becoming IA9100"
    The aerospace standard is being reissued as **IA9100** — *International
    Aerospace* — aligned with **ISO 9001:2026**. Until it is published,
    **AS9100D remains the standard you are certified to**, and this page maps
    it. For the ISO 9001:2026 base clauses, see [ISO 9001](iso9001.md).

There is no readiness percentage here: AS9100 certifies **your organisation**,
and the aerospace additions lean heavily on configuration discipline and
first-article evidence, which is where uqmes does its work.

| Standard | Version | Where uqmes sits |
|----------|---------|------------------|
| AS9100 | Rev D | Provides the production, traceability and configuration-control machinery; the programme around it is yours |
| AS9102 | Rev C | First-article inspection data is captured and reportable; the AS9102 Forms 1–3 output itself is not generated |

## Aerospace-Specific Requirements

### 8.1 Operational Planning and Control

| Clause | Requirement | Status | Evidence | Gaps / Notes |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------|-------|
| 8.1.1 | Operational risk management | 🟡 | CAPA `severity` (Critical, Major, Minor). Where the tenant keeps a CAPA approval template, a Major or Critical CAPA's plan waits for approval before corrective work and root-cause analysis start; containment never waits. The **severity chooses its approval template** (Critical, Major, or the general one as fallback), so tiered authorisation is as strict as those templates are configured | There is no risk register |
| 8.1.2 | Configuration management | ✅ | Process and document versioning (`create_new_version()`); approved records are frozen by the approval lock and change only by a new revision — see [Configuration Management](#configuration-management) |  |
| 8.1.3 | Product safety | 🟡 | A work-instruction substep can be flagged safety-critical (`is_critical`) and is shown as critical to the operator; a disposition's Critical severity marks a safety or regulatory nonconformance; quarantine holds nonconforming product | The product-safety programme itself is yours |
| 8.1.4 | Counterfeit parts prevention | 🟡 | Provenance is recorded and gated at receiving: `MaterialLot.source_type` (manufacturer / authorized distributor / independent distributor), `heat_number`, and `certificate_of_conformance`. `services/qms/receiving_inspection.py` holds a lot on `AWAITING_COC` when the item requires a certificate, on `AWAITING_HEAT_NUMBER` likewise, on `SUPPLIER_UNQUALIFIED` when the supplier isn't qualified for it, and on a missing part approval. The paperwork holds clear themselves when the certificate or heat number arrives; the others are released by a person, and the lot stays in quarantine until none is left | No authentication or test workflow specific to independent-distributor material: `source_type` is recorded, but nothing requires extra verification when it is INDEPENDENT_DISTRIBUTOR |

### 8.5 Production and Service Provision

| Clause | Requirement | Status | Evidence | Gaps / Notes |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------|-------|
| 8.5.1.1 | Equipment/tools/software | ✅ | The **Equipment + roles** capture records which machine and gauge were used against each part at a step; inspection equipment is linked to the quality report it measured (`QualityReportEquipment`), which is what the calibration exposure list reads. A reading is refused against a gauge that is out of service or failed its calibration, whether recorded singly, in bulk or from a work instruction (`gauge_refusal` in `services/mes/equipment.py`) |  |
| 8.5.1.2 | Special process validation | 🟡 | A supplier qualification can be scoped to a **special process** — heat treat, plating, NDT — with a certificate basis such as Nadcap and an expiry. An outside-process step naming a **Special process** refuses send-out to a vendor without an active qualification for it | The check runs only where the step names its special process. Validation of your own in-house special processes is not modelled |
| 8.5.1.3 | Production process verification | ✅ | `is_first_piece` flag; `requires_first_piece_inspection` on Steps; FPI workflow with scope options |  |
| 8.5.2 | Identification and traceability | ✅ | Parts have serial/ERP IDs; StepTransitionLog with operator/timestamp. **Acceptance authority media:** a signature block in a work instruction is the electronic equivalent of an inspection stamp, and the controls over it are the ones this clause asks you to document — who may sign (permissions), that a verifier cannot be the person who did the work (**enforced without exception** — the check refuses outright when verifier and submitter are the same account, and the finder is stamped by the server as the submitter), re-authentication with the signer's own password where the author requires it, attribution set by the server rather than the client, a throttle on failed attempts, and retraction only by the signer before submission or by a supervisor holding `clear_gate_signature`, with the retracted signature kept. See [Electronic Signatures](signatures.md). |  |
| 8.5.4 | Preservation | 🟡 | A storage location can be restricted to held or rejected stock only (`held_only`); shelf life is tracked on material lots, and an expired lot is held at receiving | Physical handling and packaging are yours |
| 8.5.5 | Post-delivery activities | 🟡 | Customer complaints are a CAPA type; a returned unit can be received as a core with the source *Warranty Return* | No warranty-claim or field-failure tracking beyond that |
| 8.5.6 | Control of changes | ✅ | Approved processes are frozen and change through a process change request; document revisions need a change justification; recorded measurements are never edited or deleted — a wrong reading is voided and captured again |  |

### 8.6 Release of Products

| Clause | Requirement | Status | Evidence |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------|
| 8.6 | Release | ✅ | A step can be gated so a lot does not advance until QA releases it (`requires_qa_signoff`), and the author chooses how firmly that release is signed (`qa_signoff_assurance`: check off, or signature + password). The `QaApproval` carries the signer, the server's time, the originating IP, the verification method, and a server-derived meaning — *"QA release of {step} for {work order}"*. Whoever worked the step, **on units still at the step** — signed one of its substeps, or is assigned to or completed the unit's open visit to it — is refused, and that check runs before the password, so a release cannot be bought by someone not entitled to make it.<br><br>**Scope that claim carefully in an audit.** It is a bar on releasing work you are recorded as having just done, not a guarantee that two people touched every lot: work on units that already moved past the step falls outside it, and so do voided substep completions. Where you need true independence on every lot, the control is procedural. |

!!! important "Two different controls, both called segregation"
    This page claims segregation of duties in two rows and they are **not the
    same control**. Read them separately in an audit:

    - **8.5.2, the inspection verifier.** Unconditional. A verifying signature
      is refused when the verifier is the person submitting, full stop — no
      scope, no precondition, nothing to work around.
    - **8.6, the QA release.** Bounded, as stated in that row. It bars you from
      releasing work you are recorded as having just done, which is narrower
      than "two people touched this lot".

    The wording is similar because the intent is; the enforcement is not. Do not
    cite the 8.6 bound as a limit on 8.5.2, or 8.5.2's strength as evidence for
    8.6.

## First Article Inspection (AS9102)

AS9102 requires three forms for first article approval.

uqmes does not generate the AS9102 forms. The data behind them is captured, and
a completed FAI report can be held as a controlled document (type *First Article
Inspection*) and recorded as a part approval of type FAI.

| Form | Purpose | Status | Evidence |
|------|---------|--------|----------|
| Form 1 | Part Number Accountability | 🟡 | Parts with serial/ERP IDs; process versioning; the FPI record |
| Form 2 | Product Accountability | 🟡 | Controlled documents and drawing references; material lots with certificate of conformance and heat number |
| Form 3 | Characteristic Accountability | 🟡 | Measurement definitions with nominal, tolerances and a `characteristic_number` (the drawing's balloon number); recorded results against them |

### FPI Implementation

The application supports First Piece Inspection with:

- **Step-level FPI toggle**: `Steps.requires_first_piece_inspection`
- **FPI Scope options**: PER_WORKORDER, PER_SHIFT, PER_EQUIPMENT — each a real gate: one first piece per work order, per shift day, or per machine
- **FPIRecord model**: Tracks status (NOT_REQUIRED, PENDING, PASSED, FAILED, WAIVED, VOIDED, SUPERSEDED), approval, waiver reasons
- **Batch hold**: Parts wait at step until FPI passes

**Key files**: `Tracker/models/mes_lite.py` (Steps), `Tracker/models/qms.py` (FPIRecord)

### 3D Annotation for FAI

!!! warning "Not a ballooning tool"
    Annotations on a part's 3D model mark **defect locations** against a quality
    report — a point, an error type, a severity and notes. They are not
    characteristic balloons, they carry no characteristic number, and they do
    not produce AS9102 Form 3's characteristic accountability. Do not present
    them as a substitute for a ballooned drawing.

## Configuration Management

### Process Versioning

| Feature | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Version tracking | `Processes.version`, `previous_version` FK |
| Effectivity | A version takes effect when approved (`approved_by`, `approved_at`). A temporary version, put in force by a process change request, applies to named work orders only and carries its last working day (`temporary_until`) |
| Status workflow | DRAFT → PENDING_APPROVAL → APPROVED → DEPRECATED |
| Immutability | A process is frozen once it leaves DRAFT — the approval lock (`services/core/approval_lock.py`, checked in `TenantScopedMixin.initial`) refuses a client PATCH/PUT/DELETE, and refuses `status`/`approved_by`/`approved_at` as edits. Change goes through a process change request. |

### Document Control

| Feature | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Version control | `Documents.version`, `create_new_version()` |
| Change justification | A revision is refused without `change_justification` |
| Status workflow | DRAFT → UNDER_REVIEW → APPROVED → RELEASED → OBSOLETE; release and obsolete need `release_documents` |
| Review dates | `review_date`, calculated from the document type's review period |

## Nonconformance and Disposition

### Disposition Types

| Type | Code | Use Case |
|------|------|----------|
| Use As Is | USE_AS_IS | Non-conformance doesn't affect function |
| Rework | REWORK | Can be corrected to full conformance |
| Repair | REPAIR | Corrected but may deviate (requires approval) |
| Scrap | SCRAP | Cannot be used or repaired |
| Return to Supplier | RETURN_TO_SUPPLIER | Supplier responsible |
| Not a nonconformance | NOT_NONCONFORMANCE | The finding was mistaken; closes with nothing to contain |

### Customer Approval Workflow

For dispositions requiring customer authorization:

```
┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐
│ Disposition │────▶│  Customer   │────▶│   Execute   │
│  Decision   │     │  Approval   │     │  Decision   │
└─────────────┘     └─────────────┘     └─────────────┘
```

Fields: `requires_customer_approval`, `customer_approval_received`, `customer_approval_reference`, `customer_approval_date`

## Not provided

Stated once so an audit does not look for them: AS9102 form generation, an
authentication workflow for independent-distributor material, validation of
your own in-house special processes, a risk register, warranty and
field-failure tracking, and a requirements-review record. Management review is
outside the software.
