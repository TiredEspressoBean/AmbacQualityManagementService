# Customer Portal Guide

This guide is for external customers who access uqmes to view their order status and documents.

!!! important "The portal is the Tracker page"
    Your orders live on **Tracker**, in the sidebar — not on Home. **Home is
    deliberately bare** for a customer: *"Use the sidebar to navigate to your
    workflow."* If you are looking for your orders and Home looks empty, that is
    the signpost, not a fault.

## Your Access

As a Customer user, you can:

- View your company's orders
- Track order progress
- Access shared documents
- View part information

Your access is limited to orders you own or were invited onto. It is read-only for order and production data; you can respond to approvals sent to you and invite other viewers onto an order you can see.

## Getting Started

### First-Time Login

1. You'll receive an invitation link — by email, or from your contact at the
   supplier directly, depending on how they run uqmes
2. Open the link to set up your account
3. Create a password (or use SSO if available)
4. Log in to the portal

### Portal Overview

When you log in, you'll see:

- **Tracker**: Your orders and their status

Documents for your orders are accessible from within the order details on Tracker.

## Viewing Your Orders

### Order List

1. Navigate to **Tracker**
2. See all your company's orders
3. Each card shows:
   - The order's name
   - Its delivery date, with days remaining — or a red **Overdue** badge once it
     has passed
   - The stage it is at now
   - A progress bar of stages completed out of the total

### Order Progress

The progress bar counts **stages**, not parts: *3 / 11* means three of the
order's eleven production stages are done. A stage counts as done once no part
is still sitting at it and at least one has moved past.

### Order Details

Click an order to see:

- Order information
- Part list and status
- Associated documents
- Key dates

## Understanding Status

### Order Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **RFI** | Request for information; not yet a live order |
| **Pending** | Accepted, not yet started |
| **In progress** | Work is underway |
| **On hold** | Temporarily paused |
| **Completed** | All parts finished |
| **Cancelled** | Order will not be fulfilled |

### Part Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **In Progress** | Moving through production |
| **Awaiting QA** | Waiting on an inspection |
| **Completed** | Finished |
| **Quarantined** | Held pending a quality decision |
| **Shipped** | Dispatched |

Parts carry more statuses than these — rework, scrap, outside processing and
core states among them. These are the ones you will most often see.

## Finding an order

The Tracker has **no search box and no filters**. It lists your own orders —
and only yours — as cards you scroll, loading more as you go. Find the order by
its name; your browser's find-in-page (Ctrl+F) searches the cards already
loaded.

## Notifications

You may receive notifications about your orders — a shipment going out, for
example.

**Whether you get them, and which ones, is set up by your supplier.** There is
no notification preference page for you: the rules live on their side and are
routed to the contacts they hold for your company. Notifications about customer
orders are **off unless your supplier turns them on**, so silence may mean no
rule rather than no news.

If you want to be told about something, or told at a different address, ask your
contact there.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I track a specific order?

The Tracker has no search. It lists only your own orders, as cards you scroll,
so find the order by its name and click it to open the detail. Your browser's
find-in-page (Ctrl+F) works on the names of the cards already loaded.

### Why can't I see certain information?

You only have access to information your supplier has shared with you. Internal production details are not visible.

### How do I get more documents?

Contact your supplier representative to request additional documentation.

### Who do I contact with questions?

Contact your supplier's customer service or sales representative for order-specific questions.

### Can I update order information?

No, the portal is read-only. Contact your supplier to request changes.

## What You Can't Do

As a customer portal user:

- You cannot edit orders or parts
- You cannot see CAPAs, NCR analysis or other internal quality investigations
- You cannot access another customer's data — every list is filtered to orders you
  own or were invited onto

!!! note "You can see quality reports on your own parts"
    Inspection records and their defects are visible to you where they sit on a part
    belonging to one of your orders. What stays internal is the investigation around
    them — CAPAs, corrective actions and NCR analysis.

!!! note "A few things you can write"
    The portal is read-only for *order data*, but not for everything. You can
    **respond to an approval** sent to you — which records an electronic signature
    against your name — and you can **invite another viewer** onto an order you
    can see, so a colleague can follow it too. Inviting them gives them a portal
    account if they do not already have one, and sends them an invitation.

## Getting Help

### Order Questions

Contact your supplier's:
- Sales representative
- Customer service
- Order management team

### Portal Access Issues

Contact your usual contact at the supplier. Portal access, what you can see,
and who your login belongs to are all configured on their side — there is no
separate portal support line.

### Technical Problems

- Try refreshing the page
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser
- Contact your supplier's support
